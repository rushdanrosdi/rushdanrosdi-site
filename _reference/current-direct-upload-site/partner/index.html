Partner FAQ

Common questions before introducing this type of diagnostic into a client relationship.

Is this the same as SEO?

No. Traditional SEO focuses mainly on visibility in search results. This scan focuses on how AI systems answer buyer questions, mention brands, cite sources and build vendor shortlists.

Can the partner stay in front of the client?

Yes. The default model is partner-controlled. I can work privately behind the scenes or support a controlled briefing if needed.

Can this be white-labelled?

Yes. The engagement can be structured as a white-label or partner-branded diagnostic, depending on the relationship and scope.

Do you need access to client systems?

Usually no for the first scan. The initial diagnostic can be run from public-facing assets, AI-answer observations and category research.
